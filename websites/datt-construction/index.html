About Us

Engineering you can trust

Datt Construction & Developers is led by Kedar Phadte, a civil engineer with a Master of Engineering (M.E.) in Structural Engineering. Every project starts with sound engineering: a safe, economical structure designed properly before a single brick is laid.

From a family home to a commercial building, we handle structural design, approvals, construction and finishing under one roof, so you deal with one accountable team from the first drawing to the day you get your keys.

  • Structural design done in-house by a qualified structural engineer
  • Quality materials, tested and checked on site
  • Clear timelines and itemised estimates with no hidden costs
  • Regular site updates through every stage of work
