# Datt Construction & Developers — Website

Website for Datt Construction & Developers, led by Kedar Phadte, M.E. (Structural Engineering).
Ivory, gold and bronze colours, with a gold city skyline and corner swooshes inspired by the company's business card.

Live: https://datt-construction.vercel.app

Open `index.html` in a browser to view it. It is a plain HTML/CSS/JS site with no build step, so it can be hosted anywhere (GitHub Pages, Netlify, etc.).

## Top background: video or animated skyline
The top section plays three background clips of Indian construction sites that fade into
each other (Noida aerial RCC slab, Noida concrete mixing, high-rise timelapse; all from
Pexels, free for commercial use). Laptops/tablets use `hero-l*.mp4`, phones `hero-p*.mp4`.
To switch back to the animated day-to-night skyline, open `index.html` and remove
`hero--video` from `<section class="hero hero--video">`.
